Larkspur support FAQ

Q: How do patients cancel an appointment?
A: From the link in their confirmation email, up to 24 hours before the visit. Inside 24 hours they call the clinic.

Q: Does Larkspur sync with Google Calendar?
A: One-way today (Larkspur to Google). Two-way sync is on the Q4 roadmap.

Q: Who handles billing questions?
A: Escalate them to Sam.
