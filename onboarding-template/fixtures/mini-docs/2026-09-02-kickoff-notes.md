Date: 2026-09-02

## Context

Larkspur is a scheduling app for small clinics: patients book online, staff manage the day from one calendar.

## Decisions

- Paid plans are billed per seat per month, at $8 per seat.
- Calendar sync with Google Calendar ships before any new integrations.
